# Global Corporate Registry Reference
# For use with the Dossier Intelligence Gem
# Last Updated: 2026-03-02

## ASIA-PACIFIC

### Singapore
- Registry: Accounting & Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA) BizFile
- URL: https://www.bizfile.gov.sg
- Data: Company registration, directors, shareholders, financial filings, company status
- Search: Company name or UEN (Unique Entity Number)
- Notes: Paid search for detailed records; basic info available free

### Hong Kong
- Registry: Companies Registry - Integrated Companies Registry Information System (ICRIS)
- URL: https://www.icris.cr.gov.hk
- Data: Company registration, directors, secretaries, charges, annual returns
- Search: Company name or company number (CR number)
- Notes: Some searches require HKD fee; free basic company name search available
- Also: SFC Licensed Persons Register - https://www.sfc.hk/en/Regulatory-functions/Intermediaries/Licensing/Register-of-licensed-persons-and-registered-institutions

### Malaysia
- Registry: Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia (SSM) / Companies Commission of Malaysia
- URL: https://www.ssm.com.my
- Data: Company registration, directors, shareholders, business registrations
- Search: Company name or registration number
- Notes: MyCoID portal for online searches

### Malaysia (Labuan)
- Registry: Labuan Financial Services Authority (Labuan FSA)
- URL: https://www.labuanfsa.gov.my
- Data: Licensed entities, Labuan companies, Labuan trusts
- Notes: Separate from SSM; offshore financial centre jurisdiction

### Japan
- Registry: Ministry of Justice - Legal Affairs Bureau
- URL: https://www.moj.go.jp
- Data: Company registration, directors, articles of incorporation
- Notes: Searches primarily in Japanese

### Australia
- Registry: Australian Securities & Investments Commission (ASIC)
- URL: https://connectonline.asic.gov.au
- Data: Company registration, directors, officeholders, financial reports
- Search: Company name or ACN/ABN
- Notes: Basic searches free; detailed documents paid

### India
- Registry: Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)
- URL: https://www.mca.gov.in
- Data: Company registration, directors, annual filings, charges
- Search: Company name or CIN (Corporate Identification Number)

### China
- Registry: National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (NECIPS)
- URL: https://www.gsxt.gov.cn
- Data: Company registration, legal representative, shareholders, annual reports
- Notes: Interface in Chinese; consider Tianyancha or QCC for English-accessible lookups

### South Korea
- Registry: Korea Financial Supervisory Service DART
- URL: https://dart.fss.or.kr
- Data: Company filings, financial reports, material disclosures
- Notes: Listed companies; interface in Korean with some English

## EUROPE

### United Kingdom
- Registry: Companies House
- URL: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk
- Data: Company registration, directors, persons with significant control (PSC), charges, financial statements, filing history
- Search: Company name or company number
- Notes: Extensive free data; one of the most comprehensive open registries globally

### Ireland
- Registry: Companies Registration Office (CRO)
- URL: https://www.cro.ie
- Data: Company registration, directors, annual returns
- Search: Company name or company number

### Netherlands
- Registry: Kamer van Koophandel (KvK) / Chamber of Commerce
- URL: https://www.kvk.nl
- Data: Company registration, trade names, UBO register
- Search: Company name or KvK number

### Germany
- Registry: Handelsregister (Commercial Register)
- URL: https://www.handelsregister.de
- Data: Company registration, managing directors, proxy holders
- Notes: Interface in German; federal system with state courts

### Luxembourg
- Registry: Registre de Commerce et des Societes (RCS)
- URL: https://www.lbr.lu
- Data: Company registration, beneficial owners, financial filings
- Notes: Important for fund structures and holding companies

### Switzerland
- Registry: Zefix (Central Business Names Index)
- URL: https://www.zefix.ch
- Data: Company registration, registered office, purpose, persons
- Notes: Federated system; Zefix aggregates cantonal registers

## AMERICAS

### United States
- Federal - SEC EDGAR: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar
  - Data: Public company filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, proxy statements, insider trading)
  - Search: Company name, ticker, or CIK number
  - Notes: Comprehensive for publicly listed companies
- State-level: Each state has Secretary of State business search
  - Delaware: https://icis.corp.delaware.gov/ecorp/entitysearch
  - New York: https://appext20.dos.ny.gov/corp_public/corpsearch.entity_search_entry
  - California: https://bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov/search/business
  - Notes: Many US companies incorporate in Delaware regardless of operating location
- PACER (court records): https://pacer.uscourts.gov
  - Data: Federal court filings, bankruptcy records
  - Notes: Paid per-page; search is free

### Canada
- Registry: Corporations Canada (Federal)
- URL: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cc/lgcy/fdrlCrpSrch.html
- Data: Federal company registrations
- Notes: Provincial registries also exist for provincially incorporated companies

### Cayman Islands
- Registry: Cayman Islands General Registry
- URL: https://www.ciregistry.ky
- Data: Company registration, limited public information
- Notes: Limited transparency; offshore jurisdiction

### British Virgin Islands (BVI)
- Registry: BVI Financial Services Commission
- URL: https://www.bvifsc.vg
- Data: Licensed entities, limited company search
- Notes: Very limited public information; known for privacy

### Guernsey
- Registry: Guernsey Registry
- URL: https://www.guernseyregistry.com
- Data: Company registration, directors

### Isle of Man
- Registry: Isle of Man Companies Registry
- URL: https://www.gov.im/categories/business-and-industries/companies-registry/
- Data: Company registration, directors, annual returns

### Cook Islands

- URL: https://www.fsc.gov.ck

## GLOBAL AGGREGATORS

### OpenCorporates
- URL: https://opencorporates.com
- Data: Aggregated company data from 140+ jurisdictions
- Search: Company name, officer name, or jurisdiction
- Notes: Free basic search; API and bulk data available. Excellent starting point for cross-jurisdiction searches.

### LEI (Legal Entity Identifier) Lookup
- URL: https://search.gleif.org
- Data: LEI registrations, entity relationships, ownership chains
- Notes: Useful for regulated financial entities

### ICIJ Offshore Leaks Database
- URL: https://offshoreleaks.icij.org
- Data: Panama Papers, Paradise Papers, Pandora Papers entity data
- Search: Name, jurisdiction, or intermediary
- Notes: Leaked data from offshore service providers; significant for due diligence

## SEARCH TIPS

1. Always search both the individual's name AND the company name in corporate registries
2. Check for variant spellings, transliterations, and maiden/married names
3. Cross-reference directors found in one jurisdiction against other registries
4. Note registration dates - recent incorporations before major transactions may be red flags
5. Check if companies are active, dormant, or dissolved - dissolved companies with recent transactions are suspicious
6. For Asian names, search both Western order (Given Family) and Eastern order (Family Given)
7. Use OpenCorporates as a starting point, then verify in official registries

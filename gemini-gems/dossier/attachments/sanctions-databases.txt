# Sanctions, PEP & Watchlist Database Reference
# For use with the Dossier Intelligence Gem
# Last Updated: 2026-03-02

## SANCTIONS LISTS

### United States - OFAC SDN List
- Authority: U.S. Department of the Treasury, Office of Foreign Assets Control
- URL: https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov
- Coverage: Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons
- Search: Name, ID number, country, programme
- Data: Full name, aliases, DOB, nationality, passport numbers, addresses, programme basis
- Notes: Primary global sanctions list. Includes SDN, Sectoral Sanctions, Non-SDN lists.
- Update frequency: Multiple times per week

### United Nations - Consolidated Sanctions List
- Authority: UN Security Council
- URL: https://www.un.org/securitycouncil/sanctions/un-sc-consolidated-list
- Coverage: Individuals and entities designated by UN Security Council committees
- Search: Name search available on website
- Data: Name, aliases, DOB, nationality, designation date, narrative summary
- Notes: Baseline sanctions adopted by most countries. Search via: https://scsanctions.un.org
- Update frequency: As designations are made

### European Union - Consolidated Sanctions List
- Authority: European Commission
- URL: https://www.sanctionsmap.eu
- Coverage: EU autonomous sanctions and UN-derived sanctions
- Search: Interactive map and searchable database
- Data: Name, DOB, designation basis, relevant legal acts
- Notes: Includes both UN-derived and EU autonomous measures
- Also: EU Financial Sanctions Database - https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/fsd/fsf

### United Kingdom - OFSI Consolidated List
- Authority: HM Treasury, Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation
- URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/financial-sanctions-consolidated-list-of-targets
- Coverage: UK financial sanctions targets (post-Brexit, diverges from EU)
- Data: Name, aliases, DOB, address, designation basis
- Notes: Downloadable as XML/CSV. Search via: https://sanctionssearchapp.ofsi.hmtreasury.gov.uk

### Hong Kong - Sanctions List
- Authority: Commerce and Economic Development Bureau
- URL: https://www.cedb.gov.hk/citb/en/policy-responsibilities/sanctions.html
- Coverage: UN Security Council sanctions as implemented in Hong Kong
- Notes: Primarily mirrors UN sanctions

### Singapore - MAS Sanctions
- Authority: Monetary Authority of Singapore
- URL: https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/anti-money-laundering/targeted-financial-sanctions
- Coverage: UN Security Council designations, MAS-specific designations
- Notes: Published via MAS Notices to Financial Institutions

### Australia - DFAT Consolidated Sanctions List
- Authority: Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade
- URL: https://www.dfat.gov.au/international-relations/security/sanctions/consolidated-list
- Coverage: UN and Australian autonomous sanctions
- Data: Downloadable consolidated list

## PEP (POLITICALLY EXPOSED PERSONS) DATABASES

### What is a PEP?
A Politically Exposed Person is an individual who holds or has held a prominent public function. PEP status is a risk indicator, not an accusation - it means enhanced due diligence is required.

### PEP Categories:
- Heads of state/government
- Senior politicians and legislators
- Senior government/judicial/military officials
- Senior executives of state-owned enterprises
- Important political party officials
- Family members and close associates of the above

### Publicly Available PEP Sources:
1. CIA World Leaders: https://www.cia.gov/resources/world-leaders/
   - Current heads of state, cabinet members for all countries
2. Every Politician: https://everypolitician.org
   - Database of politicians worldwide
3. Wikidata: https://www.wikidata.org
   - Structured data on political figures
4. National government websites
   - Parliament/legislature member lists
   - Cabinet and ministry websites
   - Judicial appointment records
5. Wikipedia
   - Biographical articles on political figures
   - Cross-reference against official sources

### PEP Assessment Approach:
1. Search target name against known political figures in relevant jurisdictions
2. Check if target holds/held government office, legislative seat, judicial appointment, or military command
3. Check if target is a board member or senior executive of a state-owned enterprise
4. Check if target is a known family member or close associate of a PEP
5. Note the jurisdiction, level of office, and recency (current vs. former)

## INTERNATIONAL WATCHLISTS & DATABASES

### ICIJ Offshore Leaks Database
- URL: https://offshoreleaks.icij.org
- Coverage: Panama Papers (2016), Paradise Papers (2017), Pandora Papers (2021), and other leaked datasets
- Data: Offshore entities, officers, intermediaries, addresses
- Search: Name, country, jurisdiction, data source
- Notes: Appearance does not indicate wrongdoing. Many legitimate reasons for offshore structures. However, useful for identifying undisclosed connections.

### World Bank - Listing of Ineligible Firms & Individuals
- URL: https://www.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement/debarred-firms
- Coverage: Firms and individuals debarred from World Bank-financed projects
- Data: Name, country, grounds for debarment, debarment period
- Notes: Cross-debarment with other multilateral development banks (AfDB, ADB, EBRD, IDB)

### Interpol - Public Notices
- URL: https://www.interpol.int/How-we-work/Notices
- Coverage: Red Notices (wanted persons), public database
- Search: Name, nationality, age range
- Notes: Only a subset of notices are public. Absence from public database does not mean no notice exists.

### FBI - Most Wanted
- URL: https://www.fbi.gov/wanted
- Coverage: FBI wanted persons across categories
- Notes: US-specific but globally relevant for serious crimes

### Europol - Most Wanted
- URL: https://www.europol.europa.eu/crime-areas-and-statistics/most-wanted-fugitives
- Coverage: Europe's most wanted fugitives
- Notes: EU-focused

## REGULATORY ENFORCEMENT DATABASES

### Singapore - MAS Enforcement Actions
- URL: https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/enforcement/enforcement-actions
- Data: Civil penalties, criminal convictions, prohibition orders, composition penalties

### Hong Kong - SFC Enforcement
- URL: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Regulatory-functions/Enforcement
- Data: Enforcement actions, disciplinary proceedings, prosecutions

### Malaysia - SC Malaysia Enforcement
- URL: https://www.sc.com.my/regulation/enforcement
- Data: Administrative actions, civil actions, criminal prosecution

### UK - FCA Enforcement
- URL: https://www.fca.org.uk/news/search-results?np_category=enforcement&start=1
- Data: Final notices, decision notices, supervisory notices, criminal prosecutions

### US - SEC Enforcement
- URL: https://www.sec.gov/litigation/litigations.htm
- Data: Administrative proceedings, civil actions, trading suspensions

### US - DOJ FCPA Actions
- URL: https://www.justice.gov/criminal/criminal-fraud/related-enforcement-actions
- Data: Foreign Corrupt Practices Act enforcement actions
- Notes: Critical for anti-bribery due diligence

## SEARCH METHODOLOGY

### For Each Target, Check:
1. All relevant sanctions lists based on nationality and jurisdictions of operation
2. PEP status based on known public roles
3. ICIJ Offshore Leaks for undisclosed offshore connections
4. World Bank debarment list
5. Interpol public notices
6. Regulatory enforcement databases for relevant jurisdictions

### Interpreting Results:
- MATCH on sanctions list = Immediate red flag, requires legal review
- PEP status = Enhanced due diligence required, not inherently negative
- ICIJ Offshore Leaks match = Requires context assessment; presence alone is not negative
- World Bank debarment = Significant integrity concern
- Regulatory enforcement = Assess severity, recency, and remediation
- CLEAR on all searches = Note as "No adverse findings in public databases as of [date]"

### Limitations:
- These are publicly available databases only
- Commercial screening services (World-Check, Dow Jones, LexisNexis) have broader coverage
- Negative results do not guarantee absence from restricted lists
- Sanctions lists change frequently - results are point-in-time
- Some jurisdictions have limited public enforcement disclosure
